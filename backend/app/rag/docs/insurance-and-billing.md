# Insurance and Billing

## Insurance providers
We direct bill Sun Life, Manulife, Canada Life and Green Shield. For other providers, we can give you a detailed receipt to submit yourself.

## What insurance covers
Coverage depends on your plan. You pay your deductible, any co-payment and any amount above your plan's limits at the time of your visit.

## Estimates
Ask the front desk for a treatment estimate before major work. We send a pre-authorization to your insurer and usually receive a reply within 2 business days.

## Receipts and claims
Receipts for tax and insurance purposes are available at checkout or by request. Please bring your insurance card to every new-patient visit and tell us when your coverage changes.
