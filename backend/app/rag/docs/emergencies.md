# Dental Emergencies

## Same-day emergency care
Call the office as early as possible for severe tooth pain, a broken tooth or a lost filling. We keep a few emergency slots open each weekday for same-day care.

## Knocked-out tooth
If an adult tooth is knocked out, pick it up by the crown and not the root. Keep it in milk or hold it gently in your cheek, and get to us within 30 minutes if possible.

## Go to the hospital
Facial swelling with fever, trouble breathing or swallowing, or heavy bleeding that does not stop needs the emergency room or 911, not a dental appointment.

## After hours
Outside office hours, leave a message on our main line. Messages are checked the next morning.
