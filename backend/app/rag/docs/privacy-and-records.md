# Privacy and Records

## Confidentiality
We treat your health information as confidential. We share it only with your consent or where the law requires.

## Requesting records
You can ask for a copy of your records or x-rays at the front desk. We aim to provide them within 30 days.

## Reminders
We send appointment reminders only by the contact method you gave us, and you can ask us to change or stop reminders at any time.
