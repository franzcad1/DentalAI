# Teeth Whitening

## In-office whitening
In-office whitening takes 60 to 90 minutes and costs $450. Most patients see results of several shades in one visit.

## Take-home trays
Custom take-home whitening trays cost $300 and are used for about 2 weeks.

## Insurance and sensitivity
Whitening is cosmetic and is not covered by insurance. Temporary tooth sensitivity is common, and a desensitizing gel can help.
