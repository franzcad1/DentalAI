# Your First Visit

## What to bring
Bring photo ID, your insurance card and a list of any medications you take. Arrive 15 minutes early to complete the health history form.

## What happens
The first visit takes 60 to 75 minutes. It includes a full exam, x-rays if needed, a discussion of your dental history and, when time allows, a cleaning.

## Anxious patients
Tell us if dental visits make you nervous. We can slow down, explain each step and take breaks whenever you need.
