# Cancellation and Late Policy

## Notice required
Please give at least 24 hours notice if you need to cancel or reschedule an appointment. This lets us offer the time to another patient.

## Late cancellation fee
Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours notice are charged a $50 late cancellation fee. The fee is not covered by insurance.

## No-shows
A missed appointment with no call is charged a $75 no-show fee. After three no-shows within 12 months, we ask for a card on file to book further appointments.

## Rescheduling
You can reschedule by phone or through our booking assistant. A same-day reschedule is possible when another slot is open, and the late fee is waived if the new time is confirmed before the original appointment.
