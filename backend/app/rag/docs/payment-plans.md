# Payment Plans and Accepted Payments

## Payment plans
For treatment totalling more than $500, we offer an interest-free payment plan of up to 6 monthly payments. A 20 percent deposit is due at the first treatment visit.

## Accepted payment methods
We accept debit, Visa, Mastercard and e-transfer. We do not accept cheques or cash over $200.

## Approval
Payment plans are set up with the front desk before treatment starts. Missed plan payments may pause further non-urgent treatment.
