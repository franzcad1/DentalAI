# Fillings and Crowns

## Fillings
A filling visit takes 30 to 60 minutes. The area is frozen with local anesthetic, and numbness usually lasts 2 to 3 hours. Avoid chewing on the numb side and avoid hot drinks until the feeling returns.

## Crowns
A crown takes 2 visits about 14 days apart. At the first visit we prepare the tooth and place a temporary crown. At the second visit the permanent crown is fitted.

## Temporary crown care
Avoid sticky or hard foods with a temporary crown, floss carefully by sliding the floss out sideways, and call us if the temporary crown comes off.
