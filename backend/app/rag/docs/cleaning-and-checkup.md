# Cleanings and Checkups

## How often
Most patients should have a checkup and cleaning every 6 months. Patients with gum disease or a high cavity risk may be advised to come every 3 to 4 months.

## How long it takes
A routine cleaning takes 45 to 60 minutes. A hygienist removes plaque and tartar and polishes your teeth, and the dentist then checks for cavities and gum problems.

## Preparing
No special preparation is needed. Brush and floss as usual before your visit. Let us know if your gums are sore so the hygienist can be gentle.
